Why an arrow may stand still

The green mean voltage vector stays still for a constant command. The amber switching vector jumps between active and zero vectors during a PWM period. Dead time and gate inhibit have no ideal vector. Very small captured currents hide flow dots. This inspection does not advance the motor, model Vgs, or integrate switching ripple.
