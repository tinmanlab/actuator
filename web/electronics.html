Where smoothing happens: winding inductance shapes physical current. The sensor has an analog low-pass filter; this implementation then adds capture noise and ADC quantization. A DAC may set a comparator threshold; it does not smooth the motor power current. Lower analog bandwidth does not remove noise added after that filter.

Torque–RPM and qualified efficiency

28 independent, cold-start averaged-inverter runs: 2 / 5 / 10 / 15 A at 0–3600 motor RPM. No gearbox. Last 20 ms of each 120 ms run supply time-weighted means.

Gaps mean unqualified, not zero efficiency. A point needs positive input/output power, current tracking, no fault, low stored-energy rate and 0 < η ≤ 1. Voltage saturation, copper/inverter/friction losses and numerical residual are retained in CSV. Omitted iron, switching-energy and auxiliary losses make this unsuitable as a product rating or continuous-torque map.
